## Logo Overlay Instructions

### General Guidelines for All Posts

The ASMR CCTV Trading logo should be treated as a **watermark or brand stamp** — present enough to build recognition, unobtrusive enough not to distract from the content.

---

### Using Canva (Recommended for most posts)

1. Upload your ASMR CCTV Trading logo PNG (preferably with transparent background) to **Canva Uploads**.
2. Drag it onto your design as the final layer above all other elements.
3. Resize to approximately **12–18% of the total canvas width** depending on post type.
4. **Placement recommendations:**
   - Sales/informational posts (Day 3, Day 6): Top-right or center bottom — more prominent
   - Humor/meme posts (Day 2, Day 4, Day 7): Bottom-right corner — small and watermark-style
   - Product/brand posts (Day 1, Day 5): Bottom-right corner at 70% opacity
5. To reduce opacity: click the logo element → click the **Transparency** slider (sun icon) → set to **60–75%** for a watermark effect.
6. If your logo is dark: use the **Color** adjustment in Canva to switch to a white version, or upload a pre-made white/light logo variant.

---

### Using Adobe Photoshop

1. Open the finished base image.
2. Place logo via **File > Place Embedded** — this keeps it as a Smart Object.
3. Resize and position in desired corner (hold Shift to maintain aspect ratio).
4. Set layer **Blending Mode** to: **Screen** (if logo is white/light), **Multiply** (if logo is dark/black), or **Normal** at 60–75% opacity for a standard watermark.
5. To create a subtle drop shadow: Layer Style → Drop Shadow → Distance 3px, Spread 0%, Size 5px, Opacity 30%.
6. Flatten and export as JPG (85–95% quality) or PNG.

---

### Using CapCut (For Reel thumbnails — Day 7)

1. Import the base image as a background layer.
2. Tap **Sticker** or **Overlay** → import your logo PNG from your gallery.
3. Resize to approximately 15% of the screen width.
4. Drag to the top-left corner (standard Reel thumbnail logo position).
5. Tap the logo layer → **Opacity** → set to 70–80%.
6. Export as a still image (PNG for best quality) or as the first frame of your Reel.
