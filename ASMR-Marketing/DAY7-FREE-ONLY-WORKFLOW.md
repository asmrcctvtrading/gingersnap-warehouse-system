- Check HubSpot for new leads (Awareness stage)

- Send WhatsApp to 5–10 new leads (use Template 1 or custom welcome)

- Log in HubSpot: "WhatsApp sent at 9:15 AM"

- Log responses in HubSpot deal activity

## HubSpot Pipeline (6 Stages)

```
Awareness → Demo Requested → Quote Sent → Negotiating → Won → Lost
   ↑
New leads from
Facebook/website
```

**What goes in each stage:**
1. **Awareness:** Fresh lead (no contact yet)
2. **Demo Requested:** Customer agreed to demo/site visit
3. **Quote Sent:** Sent price estimate via email
4. **Negotiating:** Customer has questions, back-and-forth
5. **Won:** Customer signed contract + scheduled installation
6. **Lost:** Customer declined, not interested, budget issue

### Option A: WhatsApp Business App (Easiest)

2. Create Business account (name: ASMR CCTV Trading)
3. Create message templates (pre-written responses)

**Cons:** Manual (one at a time), not scalable to 1000s/month

- Cost: ~₱500–1000/month (when you scale)
- Skip for now (revisit Month 2 if needed)

**Recommendation:** Start with Option A (WhatsApp Business app). Upgrade to API in Month 2 if sending 100+ messages/week.
