## Step 6: Email Tracking Setup (5 minutes)

1. Left menu → **"Settings"** (gear icon, bottom left)
2. Click **"Account settings"**

### 6.2 What This Does

- You see check mark (✓) if they open it
- You see what links they click

## FAQ

**Q: Can I delete a contact?**
A: Yes, but don't. Mark as "Do not contact" instead.

**Q: Can multiple team members use same HubSpot account?**
A: You can, but better to create separate users (HubSpot free tier: 1 user, then upgrade if adding more).

**Q: How do I know if customer opened my email?**
A: Open the deal → scroll to "Activities" → look for email with check mark (✓).

**Q: How do I export all contacts/deals?**
A: Contacts list → Click "..." (three dots) → "Export" → choose CSV format.

**Q: What happens to old deals?**
A: Keep them! Move to "Won" (if customer installed) or "Lost" (if declined). Shows your pipeline history.
