## B. Posting Strategy

### Best Times to Post (Philippines Time - PHT / UTC+8)

#### LinkedIn (B2B Decision Makers)
- **Primary window:** 8:00 AM - 9:30 AM PHT
  - Professionals checking email before meetings
  - Decision-makers reading industry content
  
- **Secondary window:** 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM PHT
  - End-of-day professional browsing
  - Wrap-up before leaving office

- **Avoid:** Weekends (minimal B2B activity)

- **Post Frequency:** 3-4 posts per week
  - Mix: 2 educational, 1 testimonial, 1 service/thought leadership

#### Facebook (Local Community & Customers)
- **Primary window:** 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM PHT
  - Lunch break scrolling
  - Local business owners browsing
  
- **Secondary window:** 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM PHT
  - Afternoon downtime
  - After-school parents browsing
  
- **Evening push:** 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM PHT
  - Weekend planning/leisure browsing

- **Post Frequency:** 4-5 posts per week
  - Mix: 2 service highlights, 1 testimonial, 1 educational, 1 culture/announcement

#### Twitter/X (Real-Time Updates & Industry News)
- **Prime windows:** 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 5:00 PM PHT
  - Morning momentum
  - Lunch engagement
  - Evening wrap-up

- **Peak engagement:** Weekday mornings 10:00-11:00 AM
  - Professionals seeking daily industry updates

- **Post Frequency:** 3-4 posts per week
  - Mix: 1 educational, 1 testimonial, 1 service, 1 real-time/reactive content

### Platform-Specific Best Practices

#### LinkedIn
- **Format:** Longer-form posts (300-500 characters ideal)
- **Tone:** Professional, expert, data-driven
- **Content:** Educational posts perform best (79% engagement rate)
- **Use:** Links to blog articles, case studies, whitepapers
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 professional hashtags (#CCTV #Security #Philippines #BusinessIntelligence)
- **Engagement:** Respond to all comments within 4 hours (builds algorithm favor)
- **Visual:** High-quality infographics or professional photography

#### Facebook
- **Format:** Conversational, 150-300 characters (short attention span)
- **Tone:** Friendly, approachable, community-focused
- **Content:** Testimonials + service promotions perform best (local engagement)
- **Use:** Local news angles, community safety, family protection
- **Hashtags:** 5-8 hashtags (#ASMRCCTV #Philippines #LocalBusiness #SafetyFirst)
- **Engagement:** Respond to comments within 2 hours (highest priority channel)
- **Visual:** Behind-the-scenes, installation photos, happy customers

#### Twitter/X
- **Format:** Punchy, 140-280 characters (get to point fast)
- **Tone:** Quick tips, industry news, slightly more casual
- **Content:** Educational tips + real-time reactions
- **Use:** Quote other security industry voices, react to news, share industry stats
- **Hashtags:** 2-3 hashtags (#CCTV #Philippines #SecurityNews)
- **Engagement:** Respond within 1 hour if possible (real-time conversation)
- **Visual:** Simple graphics, stat cards, quick comparison charts

## E. Automation Setup Guide

### Buffer Configuration (Recommended Tool for ASMR)

**Why Buffer?**
- Simple CSV import from calendar
- Scheduling across 3 platforms simultaneously
- Built-in analytics (track what works)
- Team collaboration features
- Affordable (Pro plan ~$15/month per platform)

#### Step 1: Initial Setup
1. Go to **buffer.com**
2. Sign up with business email (asmrcctvtrading@gmail.com)
3. Select "Creator" or "Business" plan
4. Add the 3 platforms:
   - LinkedIn (business page)
   - Facebook (business page)
   - Twitter/X (business account)

#### Step 2: Authentication
1. Click "Connect" for each platform
2. Authorize Buffer to post on your behalf
   - LinkedIn: Requires business page access
   - Facebook: Requires admin role on page
   - Twitter/X: Requires API access (slightly more setup)
3. Test with 1 post to ensure all platforms active

#### Step 3: Import Calendar into Buffer

**Method 1: Manual Entry (Takes 2-3 hours for 12 weeks)**
1. Dashboard → Click "Compose"
2. Enter post text
3. Add image/link
4. Select platform(s)
5. Click "Add to Queue" → Choose specific date/time
