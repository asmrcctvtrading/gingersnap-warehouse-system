## Executive Summary

ASMR CCTV Trading has a publicly visible Facebook page with 170 followers and an active but irregular posting history. The page launched publicly in September 2025, coinciding with their official website launch at asmrcctvtechnologies.com. All recent posts are in English, with customer interaction happening in Taglish. The page shows real installed work with photos and has a strong technical hashtag presence, but engagement is low (single-digit reactions per post), follower count is nascent, and no promotional campaigns or customer testimonials are present. Ten reels exist with modest view counts (177–634 per reel). The most significant opportunity is creating consistent Tagalog/Taglish content that speaks to the local San Mateo / Rizal community and addresses the visible customer interest in services beyond CCTV, such as attendance/RFID systems and network setup.

## Content Gaps

1. **No Tagalog/Taglish posts** — All written content is in English, while comments show the local audience communicates in Taglish. San Mateo, Rizal is a predominantly Filipino-speaking community.

2. **No promotional content** — No service pricing (even ranges), package deals, or seasonal promotions have been posted. The one customer inquiry about installation pricing was handled only via comment and Viber redirect.

3. **No customer testimonials or case studies** — Despite completed projects in Pililla, Rizal, and Valenzuela City, no formal testimonial posts or before/after comparisons are shown.

4. **Public Address System, Solar Power, and Fiber Cabling are not represented in posts** — These services appear on the cover photo but have no associated post content.

5. **PC Assembly, Formatting, and Printer Services not demonstrated** — These retail/repair services have no post evidence.

6. **IT Retail category not shown** — No product display posts (cameras, NVRs, accessories, cables).

7. **No before/after installation content** — Posts show completed installations but no "before" state or comparison, which would strengthen the perceived value.

8. **Reel topics are unknown** — All 10 reels have modest viewership but their content could not be verified without login. Reel content strategy is not assessable.

9. **Website not linked in About page** — asmrcctvtechnologies.com is referenced in posts but does not appear in the "Websites and social links" section of the About page.

10. **No educational/tips content** — No posts explaining CCTV types, how to choose an NVR, what SD-WAN is, how to protect your home network, etc. This type of content builds authority.

11. **Systems Development service under-promoted** — The Warehouse Management System post exists but lacks hashtags and had zero visible reactions; this high-value service deserves more attention.

12. **No FAQ or inquiry-response posts** — The RFID attendance inquiry from a potential customer suggests there is audience interest in unexplored services; these conversations should become posts.

## 90-Day Content Calendar Suggestions

**Goal:** Build local awareness in San Mateo/Rizal, demonstrate expertise, drive inquiries via messenger and website.

### Theme 1: Project Showcase (Week 1 of each month)
- Post type: Photo carousel (4–8 photos: before, during, after installation)
- Caption: Mix of English and Tagalog — describe the problem, the solution, and the outcome
- Services to cover: CCTV, network cabling, PA system, solar installation
- Example headline: "Bagong CCTV System — Installed na! 📍 [Location], Rizal"
- CTA: "Makipag-ugnayan sa amin para sa libreng site assessment."

### Theme 2: Service Spotlight (Week 2 of each month)
- Post type: Graphic + short text (Tagalog/Taglish)
- Rotate through: PTZ cameras, Hikvision products, SD-WAN, fiber cabling, solar power, PA system, PC assembly
- Example headline: "Ano ang PTZ Camera? Bakit kailangan mo nito para sa iyong negosyo?"
- Purpose: Educational; builds trust and SEO discoverability via hashtags

### Theme 3: Client Story / Testimonial (Week 3 of each month)
- Post type: Quote graphic + installation photo
- Ask completed clients (Pililla, Rizal / Valenzuela City) for a one-sentence testimonial
- Caption: "Nagpapasalamat kami kay [First name] para sa kanilang tiwala sa ASMR CCTV Trading."
- Hashtags: #customerreview #trustedpartner + location hashtag

### Theme 4: Promotion / Inquiry Driver (Week 4 of each month)
- Post type: Graphic with price or package highlight, or a call-to-action post
- Example: "Free site assessment para sa CCTV installation ngayong buwan!" 
- Or: "PC assembly + formatting starting at ₱[X]. Serbisyo sa San Mateo at karatig na lungsod."
- CTA: Messenger button or website link

### Content Types to Add Immediately
1. **Cover photo update** — Replace the text-only cover with a photo of an actual installation + services overlay. Visually stronger.
2. **Add website to About page** — asmrcctvtechnologies.com must be listed under Websites & Social Links.
3. **Enable messaging** — Resolve the reported "Can't PM your page" issue. This is likely a Page settings problem (messaging disabled).
4. **Reel: CCTV installation time-lapse** — A 30–60 second reel of a site visit from assessment to completed install is high-engagement content.
5. **Reel: "Before/After" comparison** — Split screen of an empty office wall vs. completed camera installation.
6. **FAQ post series** — Answer: "Magkano ang mag-install ng CCTV sa bahay?" / "Ilang cameras ang kailangan ko?" — drives organic reach.
7. **Product highlight post** — Hikvision camera lineup, pricing range, availability; cross-link to website shop/inquiry.
8. **Systems Development post** — Repost the Warehouse Management System content with proper hashtags, CTA, and a quote from the client; this is a high-value differentiator.

### Tagalog/Taglish Content Angles
- Lead with Tagalog headline, English body, Taglish hashtags
- Use "po" and "kayo" in CTA — keeps it professional and local
- Location-tag every post to San Mateo, Rizal for local discoverability
- Example recurring CTA: "Para sa inquiry o libreng konsultasyon, mag-message kami sa Messenger o tumawag sa 09763823899."
