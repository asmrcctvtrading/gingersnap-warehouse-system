## Keyword Placement Guide

### Page Titles (Most Important)
- Include primary keyword
- Keep under 60 characters
- Include location modifier
- Example: "CCTV Systems in San Mateo, Rizal | ASMR CCTV Trading"

### Meta Descriptions (High Impact)
- Include primary keyword naturally
- Keep 155-160 characters
- Add call-to-action
- Example: "Professional CCTV installation & 24/7 monitoring for San Mateo, Rizal. Same-day service available. Call for quote."

### H1 Heading (Critical)
- One H1 per page
- Include primary keyword
- Should match title closely
- Example: "Professional CCTV Systems & Surveillance in San Mateo"

### H2 Headings (Important)
- 2-4 H2s per page
- Include secondary keywords
- Should be descriptive
- Examples:
  - "Our CCTV Services in San Mateo"
  - "Why Choose ASMR CCTV Trading?"
  - "Service Areas in Rizal & Ampid-1"

### First Paragraph (High Weight)
- Include primary keyword in first 100 words
- Use natural language (not keyword stuffing)
- Introduce what page is about
- Example: "At ASMR CCTV Trading, we provide comprehensive CCTV and surveillance solutions for businesses and residences in San Mateo, Rizal."

### Image ALT Text
- Describe image content
- Include relevant keyword when natural
- Keep under 125 characters
- Example: "CCTV camera installation in San Mateo office"

### Internal Links (SEO Juice)
- Link service pages from homepage
- Use keyword-rich anchor text
- Example: `<a href="/services/cctv-san-mateo.php">CCTV Systems in San Mateo</a>`

### Body Content
- Naturally weave keywords throughout
- Aim for 2-3% keyword density (not over)
- Use variations and related terms
- Never sacrifice readability
