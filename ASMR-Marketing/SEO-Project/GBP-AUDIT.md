## 6. Priority Recommendations

Listed in order of estimated impact on local search visibility:

### Priority 1 — IMMEDIATE (Week 1)
1. **Fix the GBP Primary Category.** Change from "Computer store" to "Security system installer" or "CCTV installer" as primary category. Add secondary categories: "Security system supplier," "Computer repair service," "Networking service." This is the single highest-impact change for appearing in CCTV service searches.

2. **Link the website to GBP.** Add `https://asmrcctvtechnologies.com` to the GBP listing. This is currently listed as "missing information" on the GBP itself.

3. **Fix the lead photo.** The apparent Dunkin' Donuts storefront photo is the first image customers see. Replace with a professional photo of the shopfront, logo, or team. Upload properly attributed business photos.

### Priority 2 — SHORT TERM (Weeks 2–4)
4. **Actively solicit Google reviews.** Send past customers a direct GBP review link. Even 5 verified reviews would move ASMR from the absolute bottom of local rankings to competitive parity with mid-tier local competitors. Target: minimum 10 reviews within 60 days.

5. **Add a complete business description to GBP.** Write a 500–750 character description covering services (CCTV installation, networking, IT support), location, founding year (2022), and a call to action.

6. **Add all services to GBP.** Use the GBP Services section to list: CCTV Installation, IP Camera Setup, Network Installation, IT Support, CCTV Maintenance, Biometric Installation, etc.

7. **Start GBP Posts.** Publish at minimum one post per week — completed project photos, product highlights, or promotions. GBP Posts expire after 7 days; consistency signals an active business.

### Priority 3 — MEDIUM TERM (Month 1–2)
8. **Install LocalBusiness Schema on the website.** Add JSON-LD structured data with NAP matching the GBP exactly. This is Google's signal to link the website and GBP as the same business entity.

9. **Add meta descriptions to all website pages.** Currently all pages lack meta descriptions. Write unique 150–160 character descriptions per page including target keywords.

10. **Install Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Search Console.** No tracking exists at present. GA4 is needed for traffic data; Search Console is needed to monitor indexing and keyword performance.

11. **Create sitemap.xml and robots.txt.** Both return 404. A sitemap tells Google about all indexable pages; robots.txt gives crawl directives.

12. **Expand photo library.** Target 50+ photos including: shopfront exterior, interior workspace, team photos, before/after installation photos, equipment in stock, and completed project shots. Technoplete CCTV (a direct local competitor with no website) has 311 photos.
