## Executive Summary

ASMR CCTV Trading is a DTI-registered IT solutions company founded in 2022, offering a broad range of services including CCTV installation, networking, software development, hardware provisioning, biometrics, fire detection, and more. The company operates from San Mateo, Rizal, and has verifiable clients across food & beverage, education, logistics, and retail sectors.

**Key findings:**

1. **GBP Visibility Gap (Critical):** ASMR CCTV Trading did not appear in Google Maps search results for the queries "CCTV installation San Mateo Rizal Philippines," "IT support San Mateo Rizal Philippines," or "networking solutions San Mateo Rizal Philippines." This is the single largest SEO gap. Competitors such as Good Hand CCTV, AICA-Tech, and Technoplete CCTV all appeared in Maps results for the same queries.

2. **Strong Website Foundation:** The company has a functioning, well-structured website with clear service descriptions and a clean URL structure. The homepage title tag is descriptive but slightly redundant.

3. **Dominant National Competitor:** Good Hand CCTV (goodhand88.com) has a San Mateo branch and an Antipolo branch, 20+ nationwide locations, an optimized website, and active social content. They represent the toughest competition for CCTV-specific searches in the area.

4. **Local IT Space is Less Saturated:** For IT managed services, networking, and software development, the local competition in San Mateo/Rizal is weaker. AICA-Tech IT Solutions (aicatech.ph) is the main head-to-head IT competitor, but their GBP has no reviews and their website title lacks location keywords.

5. **Content and Long-Tail Keyword Gap:** There is no visible content marketing strategy on the ASMR website. Filipino-language and Taglish search queries are entirely unaddressed.

## Search Intent Analysis

### How Filipino customers typically search for CCTV and IT services

**Pattern 1 — Service + Location (Highest Commercial Intent)**
Customers ready to hire search with explicit geography:
- "CCTV installation San Mateo Rizal"
- "IT support Rizal Philippines"
- "networking solutions San Mateo"

This is the primary traffic and conversion opportunity. ASMR is not visible in these results on Google Maps currently.

**Pattern 2 — "Near Me" Queries (Mobile, High Intent)**
Mobile searches like "CCTV installer near me" or "IT support near me" are common in the Philippines and are resolved through Google Business Profile proximity. Without a verified and well-optimized GBP, ASMR cannot capture this traffic regardless of website quality.

**Pattern 3 — Price Research (Commercial/Informational)**
Filipino buyers commonly research cost before calling:
- "magkano ang CCTV installation" (how much is CCTV installation)
- "CCTV installation price Philippines"
- "presyo ng biometrics installation"

No content on the ASMR website addresses pricing or cost expectations. This is a content gap.

**Pattern 4 — Brand + Product Queries**
Customers who know the brand they want:
- "Hikvision CCTV installer"
- "ZKTeco biometrics installer"
- "Dahua camera installation"

ASMR is an authorized reseller/partner of Hikvision, Dahua, Cisco, Fortinet, and others, but there is no visible content targeting these brand-specific searches.

**Pattern 5 — Tagalog / Taglish Queries**
Many Filipino users search in mixed language:
- "magpainstall ng CCTV" (get CCTV installed)
- "CCTV para sa bahay" (CCTV for home)
- "IT support para sa negosyo" (IT support for business)
- "saan mag-install ng CCTV dito sa Rizal" (where to install CCTV here in Rizal)

The ASMR website has no Tagalog-language content or FAQ content addressing these queries.

**Pattern 6 — Informational / Educational**
Pre-purchase research queries are common among SMB owners:
- "ano ang pinagkaiba ng IP at analog camera" (what's the difference between IP and analog camera)
- "gaano karami kamerong kailangan ng opisina" (how many cameras does an office need)
- "paano mag-setup ng CCTV para sa maraming branches" (how to set up CCTV for multiple branches)

Content addressing these queries could attract organic traffic and build authority.

## Gap Opportunities

The following represent keyword and visibility gaps where ASMR could reasonably gain traction with focused effort.

### 1. Google Business Profile — Critical Gap
ASMR CCTV Trading did not appear in any of the Google Maps searches conducted during this audit (CCTV installation San Mateo Rizal, IT support San Mateo Rizal, networking solutions San Mateo Rizal, biometrics San Mateo Rizal). This is the top priority gap. Competitors with no website but a verified GBP still outrank ASMR for local searches.

**Action:** Verify or claim the Google Business Profile at #51 General Luna St., Ampid-1, San Mateo, Rizal. Add all service categories, upload photos of past projects, and begin actively requesting reviews from clients such as Dunkin', OLOPSC, and Chef's Secret.

### 2. Tagalog and Taglish Long-Tail Keywords
No Filipino-language content exists on the ASMR website. A significant portion of local search queries in the Philippines use Tagalog or Taglish phrasing. An FAQ page or blog section with content in Filipino would capture this unserved audience.

**High-priority Tagalog keywords:**
- "magkano ang CCTV installation sa Pilipinas"
- "paano mag-install ng CCTV sa opisina"
- "CCTV para sa negosyo San Mateo"
- "biometrics installation para sa opisina"

### 3. Service + Location Landing Pages
The current website has service pages (services.php with anchor links), but no individual pages dedicated to a single service in a specific location. Dedicated pages for "CCTV Installation in San Mateo, Rizal," "IT Managed Services in Rizal," and "Biometrics Installation Rizal" would each target a high-intent keyword cluster.

### 4. Price/Cost Content
No content on the ASMR website addresses what customers pay for their services. A transparent or illustrative pricing guide page (or FAQ section) targeting queries like "CCTV installation cost Philippines" or "IT managed services pricing Philippines" would capture mid-funnel, high-intent traffic that competitors are not serving with content.

### 5. Brand Partnership Content
ASMR is an authorized partner/reseller for Hikvision, Dahua, Cisco, Fortinet, Dell, Lenovo, Uniview, Sophos, and Juniper Networks. None of the partner brand names are being targeted as keywords in any visible content. Pages like "Authorized Hikvision Installer San Mateo, Rizal" or "Cisco Network Solutions Rizal" could rank for brand-specific installer queries.

### 6. Review Generation
Competitors like Good Hand CCTV Antipolo (49 reviews), CCTV Camera Good Hand Safety Solutions (8 reviews), and Citycure System Solutions (4.8 stars) all outrank in Maps because of review volume. ASMR has named clients including schools, F&B chains, and logistics companies — reaching out to these clients for Google reviews is a near-zero-cost opportunity.

### 7. Local Directory Listings
The ASMR brand did not appear on Yelp, ofphilippines.com, or other Philippine business directories observed during this research. Submitting listings to local directories would improve citation consistency and GBP authority.

### 8. Fire Alarm and Solar — Underserved Niches
No visible competitors in San Mateo Rizal are actively targeting "fire alarm installation" or "solar setup installation" for local search. ASMR offers both services, yet there is no content on the website that would rank for these queries.

### 9. Biometrics Gap
The biometrics search for San Mateo Rizal returned only two results: Good Hand CCTV and Kiel Biometric Security Shop (no website). There is essentially no local content competing in this category. A dedicated biometrics/access control page for San Mateo, Rizal would likely rank quickly.

### 10. Software Development Local Angle
DevStruct Company PH is the only visible local software company in San Mateo with a GBP listing. ASMR offers software development as a full service line but does not appear in any related search results. A content push around POS systems, inventory systems, and custom app development for local Rizal businesses could differentiate ASMR as the rare IT firm in the province offering both hardware/physical security AND software.

## Data Limitations

The following limitations apply to this research. All findings above are based only on observable, verifiable data.

1. **No access to actual search volume data.** Keyword competition levels and volume estimates are inferred from observed Maps results and search snippets. Tools such as Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, or SEMrush were not available for this audit. Actual monthly search volumes are unknown.

2. **Google blocked automated search.** Direct Google web search (google.com/search) returned a CAPTCHA block for all queries. All web search data was sourced from DuckDuckGo search results and Google Maps (which remained accessible). Some ranking positions or SERP features (featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, local pack compositions) could not be directly verified.

3. **GBP listing for ASMR was not found in search results.** It is possible a GBP listing exists but is unclaimed, unverified, or suppressed. This could not be confirmed without Google Search Console access.

4. **Competitor websites for DevStruct (devstruct.ph) and Network Fix IT Solutions could not be accessed** during this research session. Navigation to devstruct.ph was denied.

5. **Keyword ranking positions are not known.** This audit identifies keyword opportunities, not current ASMR rankings. A ranking audit requires Google Search Console data or a rank tracking tool.

6. **Review counts and star ratings are accurate as of 2026-10-11** but may change. Maps listings are not static.

7. **Facebook and social search signals** were not systematically assessed. Social signals can influence local brand awareness and indirectly impact search presence.
