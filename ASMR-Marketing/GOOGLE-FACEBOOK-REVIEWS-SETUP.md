3. Sign in with company email: **inquiries@asmrcctvtechnologies.com**
4. Enter business name: **ASMR CCTV Trading**
5. Address: **110 Gen. Luna Ave, Ampid-1, San Mateo, 1850 Rizal**
6. Business category: **Security System Installer** (or "Home Security Service")
7. Service area: **Nationwide (Philippines)** or select specific regions
8. Verify ownership (via phone or postcard)

### Step 2: Generate Your Google Review Link
Once profile is live, your unique link is:
```
https://g.page/asmrccvtrading/review
```
*(Replace with your actual business URL after claiming profile)*

**Where to get the exact link:**
1. Go to your Google Business Profile
2. Click "Customers" → "Reviews"
3. Click the "Share" button

## Part 2: Facebook Review Setup

### Step 1: Enable Reviews on Your Facebook Page
1. Go to your **ASMR CCTV Trading Facebook page**
2. Click **Settings** (top right, gear icon)
3. Scroll to **"Templates and Tabs"**
4. Find **"Reviews"** → toggle ON
5. (Optional) Add custom review message: "We'd love your feedback!"

### Step 2: Your Facebook Review URL
Once enabled, reviews appear at:
```
facebook.com/asmrcctvtrading/reviews
```
*(Replace "asmrcctvtrading" with your page username)*

**Or use this direct link format:**
```
https://www.facebook.com/[page-id]/reviews
```

### Step 3: Promote Reviews on Facebook
- Create a post: "Help other business owners! Drop a review → [link]"
- Post monthly (1st of each month)
- Pin to top of page for visibility

## Part 5: Monitoring & Response

### Google Reviews
1. **Check weekly:** Google My Business dashboard → "Reviews" tab
2. **Respond to ALL reviews** (positive + negative):
   - Positive: "Thank you! We appreciate your trust. Recommend us? 🛡️"
   - Negative: "We're sorry to hear that. Let's fix this. Please call [PHONE]."
3. **Track metrics:** Total reviews, average rating, trends

### Facebook Reviews
1. **Check weekly:** Facebook page → "Reviews" tab
2. **Respond within 24 hours** (shows responsiveness)
3. **Share positive reviews** in posts (tag customer if they allow)
4. **Example:** Post testimonial as carousel: "Here's what [Customer] said..."

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Google Business Profile won't verify | Contact support; verification can take 7–10 days |
| Can't find review link on Facebook | Enable reviews in page settings; refresh page |
| SMS link too long (exceeds 160 chars) | Use URL shortener (bit.ly or tinyurl.com) |
| Customer reviews slowly / none appear | Increase requests; ask verbally during/after installation |
| Negative review posted | Respond professionally within 24 hours; address concern |
