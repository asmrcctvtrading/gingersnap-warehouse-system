## Tips for Better Response Rates

### Timing
- **Morning (7–9 AM):** Highest open rate
- **Afternoon (2–4 PM):** Second best
- **Evening (6–8 PM):** Acceptable
- **Avoid:** Midnight–6 AM, late Sunday night

### Length
- Keep under 160 characters (fits on 1 WhatsApp screen)
- 2–3 short paragraphs max
- Use line breaks for readability

### Emoji Usage
- ✓ OK for WhatsApp (personal, friendly)
- ✗ NOT for SMS (doesn't render well)
- Use 2–3 max (not excessive)
- Use relevant emojis: 📱 phone, ☁️ cloud, 🎥 camera, 🛡️ security

### Personalization
- Always use customer name (shows you care)
- Reference their specific service (not generic)
- Include company phone (shows legitimacy)

### Tone
- Friendly but professional
- Short sentences
- Ask 1–2 questions (encourages reply)
- Always include call-to-action

## Batch Sending (Manual Process)

**If sending to 10+ leads:**

1. Make a list in Excel/Sheets:
   - Column A: Customer name
   - Column B: Phone number (+63...)
   - Column C: Service they inquired about

2. Copy template to notepad

3. For each row:
   - Replace [VARIABLES] with customer data
   - Open WhatsApp
   - Find customer contact
   - Paste personalized message
   - Send
   - Close

4. Log all sends in HubSpot:
   - Go to contact
   - Click "Log activity"
   - Type: "WhatsApp sent at [time]"

**Time estimate:** 30 messages = 1.5–2 hours

---

## Tracking Responses

### In WhatsApp
- Mark important replies with ⭐ (star)
- Create chat group "ASMR Leads" for your team (optional)

### In HubSpot
- When customer replies, log activity:
  - **Type:** WhatsApp message received
  - **Notes:** Customer said: "[their reply]"
  - **Action:** Next step (demo scheduled? budget issue? still interested?)
- Move deal to next stage if progress made

## If Customer Replies

**Replies you might get:**

| Reply | What It Means | Your Action |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| "Yes, interested" | Good sign | Send demo scheduling link / call |
| "What's the price?" | Hot lead | Send pricing template (from MARKETING_STRATEGY) |
| "Call me" | Wants to talk | Call within 1 hour (show responsiveness) |
| "Send quote" | Serious buyer | Send quote via email + WhatsApp link |
| "Not interested" | Lost lead | Tag: "Opted out" in HubSpot, don't contact again |
| No reply after 3 days | Lukewarm lead | Send Template 5 (re-engagement) on Day 7 |

## WhatsApp vs SMS (Key Differences)

| Factor | WhatsApp | SMS |
|--------|----------|-----|
| **Delivery** | Read receipts (✓✓ seen) | Blind (doesn't show if read) |
| **Cost** | Free | ₱0.56/message |
| **Tone** | Personal, conversational | Formal, short |
| **Emoji** | Fully supported | May not render |
| **Links** | Clickable + previews | May break |
| **Group chat** | Easy to create | Not available |
| **Response rate** | 40–60% | 20–30% |
