# Google & Facebook Review Request Workflow
**ASMR CCTV Trading — Getting Social Proof**  
**Created:** 2026-11-06 | **For:** Business Owner + Team

## Why This Matters

Reviews build trust. When a customer visits Google or Facebook, they want to know: *Have other people trusted you?* 

**Our goal for the first month:** Get **3–5 reviews** from customers who just had installations done.

This workflow automates asking for reviews 7 days after installation (when customers are happiest).

## Part A: Set Up Google Reviews

### Step 1: Create or Claim Your Google Business Profile

1. Go to **Google Business Profile** at `https://www.google.com/business/`
2. Click **Sign In** (use your personal Google account, or create one if needed)
3. Click **Manage Now** or **Create**
4. Enter business info:
   - Business Name: **ASMR CCTV Trading**
   - Address: **110 Gen. Luna Ave, Ampid-1, San Mateo, 1850 Rizal**
   - Phone: (Get from boss if not sure)
   - Website: `asmrcctvtechnologies.com`
   - Category: **Security System Installation** or **Electronics Store**
5. Verify your business (Google will send a PIN by mail or phone)
6. Click **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Google Business Profile dashboard with business info filled in]

### Step 2: Get Your Google Review Link

Once verified:

1. Go to your **Google Business Profile** dashboard
2. Click **Reviews** on the left menu
3. Click the **Share** button (looks like a link icon)
4. Copy the long link that looks like: `https://g.page/ASMR-CCTV-Trading/review`
5. **Store this link** — we'll use it in SMS and email templates

[SCREENSHOT: Google Business Profile Reviews page with Share button highlighted]

**Where to store the link:**
- Paste it in a note file on your computer
- Add it to your email signature
- Add it to WhatsApp Business auto-reply (if using)

## Part B: Set Up Facebook Reviews

### Step 1: Enable Reviews on Your Facebook Page

1. Go to your **ASMR CCTV Trading Facebook page** (ask boss for access if needed)
2. Click **Settings** (bottom left)
3. Scroll down to **Audience and Visibility**
4. Look for **Reviews** option
5. Click the toggle to **Turn on Reviews**
6. Click **Save**

[SCREENSHOT: Facebook Settings page with Reviews toggle highlighted]

### Step 2: Get Your Facebook Review Link

1. Go to your **Facebook Business Page**
2. In the menu, look for or create a **Reviews section** (should show under your business info)
3. If you don't see it, try this link pattern: `https://www.facebook.com/ASMRCCTVTRADING/reviews/` (replace with your page name)
4. Copy this link and store it like you did with Google

**Alternative:** If Facebook review link doesn't work, customers can also leave reviews by:
- Clicking **Reviews** tab on your page
- Clicking **Leave a review**

## Part F: How to Monitor & Respond to Reviews

### Check Google Reviews

1. Go to **Google Business Profile** dashboard
2. Click **Reviews**
3. Sort by **Newest** to see latest reviews
4. **Respond to every review** (even 5-stars — say "Thank you!")

[SCREENSHOT: Google Reviews dashboard with response form visible]

### Respond to Google Reviews:

1. Click **Reply** under the review
2. Type a short response (max 400 characters):
   - **For 5-star reviews:** "Thank you for the kind words! We appreciate your business and look forward to helping you again."
   - **For 4-star or lower:** "Thank you for your feedback. We'd like to make this right. Please contact us at inquiries@asmrcctvtechnologies.com or call [PHONE]."

3. Click **Post**

### Check Facebook Reviews

1. Go to your **Facebook Business Page**
2. Click **Reviews** (in left menu or on your page)
3. Read new reviews
4. **Like** or **comment** on positive reviews to show appreciation

[SCREENSHOT: Facebook Reviews section with comment form]

### Respond to Facebook Reviews:

1. Click on the review
2. Click **Reply** or **Comment**
3. Type a response:
   - **"Thank you so much! We're glad your system is working perfectly. We're here if you need anything else!"**

## Troubleshooting

**Q: "I can't find my Google Business Profile."**  
A: Go to `https://www.google.com/business/` and sign in. If you never created one, click "Create account" and follow steps above.

**Q: "My Facebook page doesn't have a Reviews section."**  
A: Make sure your page is set to **Business Page** (not Personal Profile). Check Settings → Page Type. You may need to ask Facebook support to enable it.

**Q: "I don't know my Google or Facebook password."**  
A: Click **Forgot Password** on the login page. You'll get a reset link sent to your email.

**Q: "How do I delete a review?"**  
A: You can't directly delete reviews on Google or Facebook, but you can **flag them as inappropriate** if they violate community guidelines. Google/Facebook will review your flag. For most issues, respond professionally in your reply.
