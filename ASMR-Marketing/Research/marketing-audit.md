## Recommendation 1 — Establish a Consistent Posting Schedule

**Problem:** The page appears to have posted only once since launch (September 2026 launch announcement). With 170 followers and only 1 visible post, the page is essentially dormant in the algorithm's eyes. Facebook deprioritizes pages that post infrequently, and potential clients who visit the page see no evidence of active business.

**Action:** Post 4–5 times per week minimum using a content calendar (see `Content-Calendar/week1-calendar.md`). Mix content pillars: Awareness (company intro), Education (tips), Product Spotlight, Engagement (funny/relatable), and Sales (quotation CTA). Consistency matters more than perfection — even a simple before/after project photo once a week outperforms silence.

**Expected outcome:** Pages that post 4+ times/week see 3–5x more organic reach within 30 days. This also builds algorithm trust, making future paid ads cheaper.
