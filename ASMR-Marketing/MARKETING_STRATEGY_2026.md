   - Load images at 2x pixel density
   - Use WebP format (faster loading)
   - Compress to under 200KB per image

5. **Content Reflow**
   - Stack testimonials vertically on mobile
   - Full-width pricing cards
   - Single-column layout (not side-by-side)

6. **Performance**
   - Page load target: Under 3 seconds (mobile 4G)
   - Lazy load images (load as user scrolls)
