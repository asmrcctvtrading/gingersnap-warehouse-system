# Reel Script 001 — ASMR CCTV Trading
**Title:** "Anong Nangyayari Sa Opisina Mo Habang Wala Ka?"  
**Duration:** 20–25 seconds  
**Format:** Facebook Reel (vertical 9:16, 1080x1920 preferred)  
**Status:** Draft | Last Updated: 2026-10-10  
**Tone:** Comedic hook → short educational beat → clear CTA  

---

## Concept Summary

Hook sa unang 3 segundo gamit ang relatable na "secret life ng opisina" angle. Ginaganap ang scenario kung paano mag-behave ang employees / customers / lugar habang wala ang boss — at suddenly pops in the CCTV footage. Transition to real CCTV benefits. Ends with soft sales CTA.

Walang kailangang professional actors. Puwedeng gawin ng in-house ASMR team gamit ang phone camera at basic editing app (CapCut, VN, InShot).

---

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1 — Hook (0:00 – 0:03)
**Visual:** Text on screen, bold white font on dark background. Brief suspenseful music sting or "dramatic reveal" sound effect.

**Text on screen:**  
> "Pag wala kang CCTV, ito ang scene sa opisina mo habang wala ka..." 👀

**Tip:** Hold 2–3 seconds max. Enough to read, enough to build curiosity. No voice-over yet.

---

### Scene 2 — Comedy Beat (0:03 – 0:10)
**Visual:** Short comedic clip or photo montage (or stock footage with Filipino captions)

Option A — Quick-cut montage of exaggerated "office gone wild" scenarios (can use stock clips or illustrations):
- Employee sleeping on desk
- Someone snacking at the coffee station for 10 minutes
- Person dancing alone to music

**On-screen text captions over each clip:**
- "Nagnanap ang staff... sa trabaho." 😴
- "10-minute 'merienda'" 🍔
- "'Nagtatrabaho' naman po ako, sir" 🕺

**Music:** Upbeat, comedic Filipino-flavored music (OPM funny beat or "kilig/comedic" royalty-free track)

---

### Scene 3 — Reality Check + Benefits (0:10 – 0:17)
**Visual:** Smooth transition to actual CCTV footage screenshot or ASMR installation photo. Clean text overlay.

**Voice-Over (in Filipino — warm, not robotic):**
> "Kaya nandito ang CCTV. Hindi para maniktik — para protektahan ang negosyo mo. Real-time. Sa cellphone mo. Kahit saan ka man naroroon."

**On-screen text (bold, sequential):**
- 📹 HD footage  
- 📱 Remote monitoring sa phone  
- 🌙 Night vision  
- 💾 Recorded footage backup  

---

### Scene 4 — CTA + Branding (0:17 – 0:22)
**Visual:** ASMR CCTV Trading logo on clean dark background. Contact info below. Simple animation (fade in/slide in).

**Voice-Over:**
> "Libre ang consultation. I-message mo kami ngayon sa Facebook."

**On-screen text:**
> **ASMR CCTV Trading**  
> 📱 0976-382-3899  
> 💬 Message us on Facebook  
> "CCTV na to." 📹

## Suggested Music Mood

- **Genre:** Light comedic OPM or royalty-free upbeat Filipino/Asian-flavored background music
- **Tempo:** Medium-upbeat for comedy scenes, slightly more serious/calm for the benefits section
- **Recommended sources:** CapCut library (Filipino-trend sounds), YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay free music

**Avoid:** Corporate/generic elevator music. Avoid anything that sounds like a drug commercial. Should feel like something a witty Filipino friend would play.
