## Getting Started

### What is WPMS?

WPMS (Warehouse Productivity Monitoring System) is a web-based application that helps you:

- **Track your tasks:** See what you need to do and when
- **Record your progress:** Log when you complete tasks
- **Report issues:** Notify supervisors of problems
- **View reports:** See your performance and productivity

### System Requirements

To use WPMS, you need:

- **Web Browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (recent version)
- **Internet Connection:** Broadband or WiFi connection
- **Device:** Computer, tablet, or mobile device
- **Screen Size:** At least 7 inches (mobile) recommended

### Getting Your Account

When you join the team:

1. Your manager creates your account
2. You receive an email with your login details
3. You'll get a temporary password
4. You must change it to a password you choose
5. Your account is ready to use

## Logging In

### First-Time Login

1. Open web browser
2. Go to: `https://asmrcctvtechnologies.com/warehouse-productivity/`
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your temporary password
5. Click "Sign In"
6. System asks you to create new password
7. Choose strong password (10+ characters, mixed case, number, symbol)
8. Click "Update Password"
9. You're logged in!

### Subsequent Logins

1. Go to: `https://asmrcctvtechnologies.com/warehouse-productivity/`
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Sign In"
5. You're logged in!

### Password Requirements

Your password must have:
- Minimum 10 characters
- At least 1 uppercase letter (A-Z)
- At least 1 lowercase letter (a-z)
- At least 1 number (0-9)
- At least 1 special character (!@#$%^&*)

**Examples of Strong Passwords:**
- `Warehouse2026!`
- `MyP@ssw0rd`
- `Pick#Ing123`

**Examples of Weak Passwords:**
- `password` (no uppercase, number, or symbol)
- `123456` (only numbers)
- `abcdef` (only letters)

### If You Forgot Your Password

1. Go to login page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your email address
4. Click "Send Reset Email"
5. Check your email for reset link
6. Click link in email
7. Enter new password
8. Click "Reset Password"
9. Log in with new password

**Note:** Password reset link expires after 1 hour. If you don't use it, request a new one.

### If You Can't Log In

**Problem: "Invalid Email or Password" Error**

1. Check email address is typed correctly
2. Check caps lock is off
3. Re-enter password carefully
4. Try again
5. If still not working, contact your manager

**Problem: "Account Locked" Error**

1. You tried to log in incorrectly 5 times
2. Account is locked for 15 minutes for security
3. Wait 15 minutes and try again
4. If problem continues, contact your manager

## Your Tasks

### Finding Your Tasks

**On Dashboard:**
1. Look for "My Tasks" section (top right)
2. Click "View All" to see complete list
3. Or click specific task to open it

**From Top Menu:**
1. Click "Tasks" in main menu
2. Click "My Assignments"
3. See all tasks assigned to you

### Task Statuses

Each task has a status:

| Status | Meaning | What You Do |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Assigned** | Task is waiting for you to start | Click to view details, then start |
| **In Progress** | You started this task | Continue working, mark items as done |
| **On Hold** | You paused the task (waiting for something) | Resume when ready, click "Resume" |
| **Completed** | You finished all items in task | Task moves to completed section |

### Task Details

Click on a task to see:

- **Picklist/Order Number:** Reference ID
- **Items to Pick/Pack:** What you need to retrieve or pack
- **Quantity:** How many of each item
- **Priority:** Urgency (Normal/High/Urgent)
- **Due Date:** When task needs to be complete
- **Started:** When you started (if already started)
- **Notes:** Special instructions from supervisor

### Sorting & Filtering

You can arrange your tasks by:

- **Due Date:** Most urgent first (default)
- **Priority:** High priority first
- **Status:** In Progress first
- **Time:** Oldest first (longest waiting)

Click filter icon (≡) to show only:
- Picking tasks
- Packing tasks
- By priority level
- By department

## Completing Picking Tasks

### Picking Workflow

**Step 1: Open Task**
1. Click task in your list
2. Task details appear
3. Review items you need to pick

**Step 2: Start Task**
1. Click "Start Task" button
2. Task status changes to "In Progress"
3. Timer starts (for performance tracking)
4. You can now begin picking

**Step 3: Locate Items**
1. Review first item on list
2. Go to warehouse location (noted on picklist)
3. Retrieve items
4. Verify quantity is correct
5. Check for damage (if warehouse standard)

**Step 4: Mark Item as Picked**
1. In WPMS, click checkbox next to item
2. Or scan barcode if using scanner (see below)
3. Item marked as picked (shown with checkmark)
4. System notes time item was picked

**Step 5: Repeat for Each Item**
1. Find next item on list
2. Retrieve it
3. Mark as picked in WPMS
4. Continue until all items picked

**Step 6: Complete Task**
1. Once all items picked, click "Mark Complete"
2. Task status changes to "Completed"
3. Click "Confirm" when prompted
4. Task removed from your active list
5. Timer stops (total time recorded)

### Adding Notes to Picks

If you encounter an issue:

1. Click "Add Note" for that item
2. Type description:
   - "Item not found in expected location"
   - "Quantity doesn't match (found 45, expected 50)"
   - "Item appears damaged"
3. Click "Save Note"
4. Note is visible to supervisor
5. Supervisor may reassign task or adjust details

## Completing Packing Tasks

### Packing Workflow

**Step 1: Open Task**
1. Click task in your list
2. Task details appear
3. Review items you need to pack

**Step 2: Start Task**
1. Click "Start Task" button
2. Task status changes to "In Progress"
3. Timer starts
4. You can now begin packing

**Step 3: Retrieve Picked Items**
1. Staging area shows items ready to pack
2. Verify quantity matches task
3. Check for damage from picking
4. Arrange by size/weight for packing

**Step 4: Prepare Box/Container**
1. Select appropriate box/container size
2. Add packing material if needed (foam, paper)
3. System can suggest standard box for items

**Step 5: Pack Items**
1. Place items in box
2. Add protective packing material
3. Ensure items won't shift during transport
4. Check weight is reasonable for handling

**Step 6: Generate Packing Slip**
1. In WPMS, click "Generate Packing Slip"
2. Print packing slip
3. Verify order information
4. Place in box or on exterior

**Step 7: Apply Labels**
1. Generate shipping label (if applicable)
2. Apply to box in correct position
3. Ensure barcode is readable
4. No labels overlapping important info

**Step 8: Mark Item as Packed**
1. Click checkbox for item in WPMS
2. System notes time item was packed
3. Item marked as packed
4. Move box to shipping area

**Step 9: Complete Task**
1. Once all items packed, click "Mark Complete"
2. Task status changes to "Completed"
3. Click "Confirm" when prompted
4. Task removed from active list
5. Timer stops

### Quality Assurance

After packing, QA team may:
- Check packing quality
- Verify correct items in box
- Ensure packing material adequate
- Verify labels correct and clear

If QA finds issues:
- Task moved back to "On Hold"
- Notification sent explaining issue
- You must fix and re-mark as complete

## Using the Barcode Scanner

### Scanner Basics

If your warehouse has barcode scanners:

1. **Turn on Scanner**
   - Most scanners power on automatically when connected
   - Or press power button (top/side of device)

2. **Test Connection**
   - Open WPMS task on computer/tablet near scanner
   - Scanner should be paired with your device (via Bluetooth or USB)

3. **Scan Barcode**
   - Point scanner at barcode on item
   - Pull trigger
   - Scanner beeps when successful
   - Barcode value appears in WPMS

### Using Scanner in Picking

1. Open picking task in WPMS
2. Hold scanner ready
3. For each item:
   - Find item in warehouse
   - Scan barcode on item
   - WPMS automatically checks item off list
   - Quantity decremented by 1
4. Repeat until all items picked

**If Scan Fails:**
- Barcode unclear/damaged: Manually type item number
- Scanner not working: Check battery, connection
- Wrong item scanned: Click "Undo" and rescan

### Using Scanner in Packing

1. Open packing task in WPMS
2. After packing all items:
   - Scan shipping/container barcode
   - WPMS records box number
   - Scan each item barcode to verify contents
3. System confirms all items in box
4. Mark task as complete

### Scanner Troubleshooting

**Scanner Not Scanning:**
1. Check battery level
2. Verify Bluetooth is connected
3. Try different barcode (test code)
4. Restart scanner and try again

**Barcode Not Found:**
1. Some items may not have barcodes
2. Ask supervisor for item code
3. Manually enter code in WPMS

**Wrong Item Scanned:**
1. Click "Undo" to remove scan
2. Rescan correct item
3. Or click "Edit" and change item manually

## Adding Notes & Exceptions

### When to Add a Note

Add a note if:
- Item is missing or damaged
- Quantity doesn't match expectations
- Item is in wrong location
- Special handling required
- Item has expiration date concern
- Other unusual circumstance

### How to Add a Note

**Method 1: From Task View**
1. Open task
2. Click "Add Note" button
3. Type your note (be specific)
4. Click "Save"

**Method 2: From Item Detail**
1. Click on specific item
2. Click "Add Exception"
3. Select exception type:
   - Missing item
   - Damaged item
   - Quantity mismatch
   - Other issue
4. Type additional details
5. Click "Save"

### Example Notes

**Good Examples:**
- "Item SKU123 not found in shelf A-1, checked nearby shelves"
- "Quantity is 45 units, but picklist says 50"
- "Item shows damage on corner of box"
- "Item has expiration date 2026-10-15"

**Less Helpful Examples:**
- "Something's wrong"
- "Can't find it"
- "Problem with this one"

### What Happens to Your Notes

1. Note is saved in system
2. Supervisor is notified
3. Supervisor reviews note
4. Supervisor takes action:
   - Investigates issue
   - May adjust task
   - May split task between people
   - Will contact you if needs clarification
5. You'll see update on task

## Troubleshooting

### Problems with Tasks

**Problem: I Can't Find My Tasks**

1. Make sure you're logged in
2. Click "Tasks" in top menu
3. Click "My Assignments"
4. Tasks should appear
5. If not, tasks may not have been assigned yet
6. Contact your supervisor

**Problem: Task Won't Start**

1. Refresh page (F5)
2. Try logging out and back in
3. Try different browser
4. If still not working, contact your manager

**Problem: Task Says "On Hold"**

1. Supervisor may have paused it
2. Review any notes on task (may explain why)
3. Contact supervisor to resume
4. Once resumed, can continue working

### Problems with the System

**Problem: Page Not Loading**

1. Check internet connection
2. Try refreshing page (F5)
3. Clear browser cache (Settings → Clear Browsing Data)
4. Try different browser
5. If still not working, system may be down

**Problem: Slow Performance**

1. Check internet connection speed
2. Close other browser tabs
3. Clear browser cache
4. Restart browser
5. Try accessing during off-peak hours (2-4 AM)

**Problem: Barcode Scanner Not Working**

1. Check scanner battery
2. Verify Bluetooth is connected
3. Restart scanner
4. Manually type item codes if needed
5. Contact your manager

### Problems with Your Account

**Problem: Password Not Accepted**

1. Check caps lock is off
2. Verify you're typing correct email
3. Try "Forgot Password" to reset
4. Contact your manager if still not working

**Problem: Session Expires During Work**

1. System logs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity
2. Click "Log In Again" to resume
3. Your work is saved before logout
4. For longer work, stay active by moving around system

### Tips for Success

1. **Log in at start of shift**
   - Check your tasks immediately
   - Review priorities
   - Plan your day

2. **Complete tasks in order**
   - High priority/urgent first
   - Or by due date (soonest first)

3. **Add notes early**
   - If you see an issue, report it immediately
   - Don't wait until end of shift
   - Helps team resolve issues faster

4. **Check your performance**
   - Review your reports regularly
   - See where you can improve
   - Celebrate your successes

5. **Ask for help**

6. **Take care of equipment**
   - Treat scanner gently
   - Report any damage immediately
   - Battery needs charging? Let manager know

7. **Stay safe**
   - Lift properly (knees, not back)
   - Watch for hazards
   - Ask for help with heavy items
